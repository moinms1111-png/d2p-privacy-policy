Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Mahedvia-e-Daira handles user information.

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Welcome to Mahedvia-e-Daira ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to user data when you use our mobile application.

Our No-Data-Collection Commitment Mahedvia-e-Daira does not collect, transmit to us, store on our servers, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data through the application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information or sensitive user data through Mahedvia-e-Daira.

We do not request or collect information such as:

  • Name or personal profile information.
  • Email addresses or phone numbers through the app.
  • Device location or GPS information.
  • Contacts, messages, or call information.
  • Photos, videos, or other personal files.
  • Financial, payment, or authentication information.
  • Personal app usage or advertising profiles.

Any information entered or generated during app use, if applicable, is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect personal or sensitive user data through the application, we do not use such data for advertising, analytics, profiling, personalization, or marketing.

The application is provided for its intended functionality without requiring users to submit personal information to us.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal or sensitive user data collected through the application to third parties.

The application does not use third-party services or software development kits (SDKs) to collect or transmit users' personal data.

Information voluntarily provided in an email sent to our support address is handled separately and is used only to respond to your inquiry.

We may disclose information contained in a support communication if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

4. Third-Party Services

Mahedvia-e-Daira does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services to collect or share personal user information.

We do not use third-party services to build user profiles or track users' activities across applications or websites.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not collect or maintain personal or sensitive user data through the application. Therefore, we do not maintain a user-data database or retain app-collected personal information.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we retain your message and email address only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve your inquiry, subject to applicable legal requirements.

You may contact us at the email address below to request deletion of your support correspondence.

6. Data Security

Because we do not collect or maintain personal user data through the application, we do not operate a database of personal user information.

We take reasonable precautions to protect any information voluntarily sent to us through support communications.

7. Children's Privacy

Mahedvia-e-Daira does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through a support communication, please contact us so that we can address your concern.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
